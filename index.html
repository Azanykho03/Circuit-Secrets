Field Manual

1

Play as Fiona and search CTU Danao's engineering rooms for the components listed in the dock.

2

Click or tap an object the moment you spot it — a correct find lights up and logs itself automatically.

3

Wrong clicks cost a little time, so look before you tap. It's a small penalty, not a disaster.

4

Stuck? Use a HINT to reveal the general area of one missing part. Hints are limited per room.

5

Clear every component before the timer runs out to advance to the next room.

6

Recovered clues are filed as evidence. Secure each room to unlock the next location.

7

Signal Lock shows your recovery progress in the room. Optional Overclock trades a faster timer for more points.

8

Zoom in to inspect small parts; after zooming, drag empty space to pan around the fixed workbench.

+

Keyboard operators: H deploys a hint, R scans, and P pauses the investigation.
