# Intro Artwork

Original fictional scenes created for Circuit Secrets: COMPLAB 2. The runtime uses an animated 3D introduction, with four hand-authored `*-cartoon.svg` illustrations as fallbacks. Fiona is a third-year Computer Engineering student at CTU Danao; her ordinary COMPLAB 2 practical becomes an investigation when the Circuit Secrets Protocol interrupts the class. The source frames and earlier PNG studies remain beside the runtime assets as archives.

## Cartoon Runtime Assets

- `campus-arrival-cartoon.svg`
- `capstone-team-cartoon.svg`
- `lab-blackout-cartoon.svg`
- `professor-briefing-cartoon.svg`

The scenes share the same computer-engineering lab, lighting, and character proportions. Fiona, her lab partner Marie, and an unnamed instructor use simple expressive features, rounded silhouettes, and natural colors inside the black-and-blue atmosphere. Embedded motion adds idle movement, drifting window reflections, and monitor light. The prologue advances automatically; Continue remains available for skipping ahead. The filenames and archived PNG briefs below predate the current story and are retained for asset provenance.

Composition/framing: wide horizontal 16:9, realistic eye-level view along the bench into the lab. Empty mat prominent center, empty rolling chair behind it. Lower quarter is mostly low-detail dark workbench for game subtitles. Lab, equipment and empty mat must remain readable and detailed, not silhouetted or almost black.
Lighting/mood: quiet, tense investigative moment after an interrupted capstone test, cool early-morning window daylight with the single soft emergency light. Realistic exposure, visible room geometry and equipment, dark monitors distinct from blue daylight. Cinematic but grounded.
Color palette: black/charcoal furniture, blue light, natural metallic silver instruments, yellow multimeter off to one side, red and black leads. Avoid orange/brown grading.
Constraints: original scene. No characters, no people reflected in windows, no PCB prototype on the central mat, no readable text, no logos, no watermark, no UI, no borders. Photorealistic practical university laboratory, not a painting or cartoon.
